Aathichudi

ஆத்திச்சூடி

Avvaiyar’s 109 sayings in Tamil letter order, each with its meaning in English or in Tamil.
Attributed to
ஔவையார் (Avvaiyar), the later Avvaiyar tradition
Literary form
Alphabetical moral sayings, for learning and recitation
In the catalogue
Moral and children’s works · நீதி நூல்கள்
Text
The opening invocation and 109 sayings

About the work

ஆத்திச்சூடி is a concise Tamil guide to conduct, learning, generosity, self-control and life with others. Its short lines follow the Tamil letter sequence, which makes them easy for young learners to remember. It is traditionally attributed to the later Avvaiyar, and is different from Subramania Bharathiyar’s புதிய ஆத்திச்சூடி.

The meaning beside each saying, in English or in Tamil (choose with the switch above the list), is a plain explanation, not a verse translation. Some sayings reflect the social and religious setting of their time; where a line has an old or disputed sense, the meaning is given carefully.

About the author

ஔவையார் · Avvaiyar

Avvaiyar is one of the most honoured names in Tamil literature. It belongs to women poets of more than one period: a Sangam-era Avvai whose poems appear in the early anthologies, and a later Avvaiyar associated with the short moral works children learn by heart, such as ஆத்திச்சூடி. Devotional works and many stories are also attached to the name.

கடவுள் வாழ்த்து · Opening invocation

ஆத்தி சூடி அமர்ந்த தேவனைஏத்தி ஏத்தித் தொழுவோம் யாமே.

Meaning: Let us repeatedly praise and worship the deity adorned with the aathi blossoms.

பொருள்: ஆத்தி மலர் சூடிய இறைவனை மீண்டும் மீண்டும் போற்றி வணங்குவோம்.
