Tamil Proverbs

பழமொழிகள்

Pazhamozhi, or old sayings, carry the everyday wisdom of Tamil homes. Each one here has a word-for-word translation and what it means when people say it.

  1. ஆழம் தெரியாமல் காலை விடாதே

    aazham theriyaamal kaalai vidaadhe

    Literally: Do not put your leg in without knowing the depth.

    Meaning: Understand a situation properly before you step into it.

  2. சிறு துளி பெரு வெள்ளம்

    siru thuli peru vellam

    Literally: Small drops, a great flood.

    Meaning: Little amounts of saving or effort, added up over time, become something big.

  3. கற்றது கை மண் அளவு, கல்லாதது உலகளவு

    katradhu kai man alavu, kallaadhadhu ulagalavu

    Literally: What has been learnt is a handful of sand; what has not been learnt is the size of the world.

    Meaning: However much you know, there is far more to learn, so stay humble and keep learning.

  4. யானைக்கும் அடி சறுக்கும்

    yaanaikkum adi sarukkum

    Literally: Even an elephant's foot slips.

    Meaning: Even the strongest or most skilled person can make a mistake.

  5. அடி மேல் அடி வைத்தால் அம்மியும் நகரும்

    adi mel adi vaiththaal ammiyum nagarum

    Literally: Blow after blow, and even the grinding stone will move.

    Meaning: Steady, repeated effort can shift even what seems immovable.

  6. எறும்பு ஊரக் கல்லும் தேயும்

    erumbu oorak kallum theyum

    Literally: As ants keep crawling over it, even stone wears away.

    Meaning: Small, patient efforts, kept up day after day, achieve great things.

  7. கடுகு சிறுத்தாலும் காரம் குறையாது

    kadugu siruththaalum kaaram kuraiyaadhu

    Literally: Though the mustard seed is small, its sharpness is not less.

    Meaning: Do not judge anyone by their size; small people can have great strength or talent.

  8. நிறைகுடம் தளும்பாது

    niraikudam thalumbaadhu

    Literally: A full pot does not slosh.

    Meaning: People who truly know a lot are calm and do not show off.

  9. ஐந்தில் வளையாதது ஐம்பதில் வளையுமா?

    aindhil valaiyaadhadhu aimbadhil valaiyumaa?

    Literally: What does not bend at five, will it bend at fifty?

    Meaning: Good habits are best learnt young; they are much harder to form later in life.

  10. அகத்தின் அழகு முகத்தில் தெரியும்

    agaththin azhagu mugaththil theriyum

    Literally: The beauty of the heart shows on the face.

    Meaning: What you feel inside, good or bad, can be seen in your face.

  11. காற்றுள்ள போதே தூற்றிக்கொள்

    kaatrulla podhe thootrikkol

    Literally: Winnow while the wind is blowing.

    Meaning: Make good use of an opportunity while you have it.

  12. இக்கரைக்கு அக்கரை பச்சை

    ikkaraikku akkarai pachai

    Literally: From this bank, the other bank looks green.

    Meaning: Other people's situations always seem better than our own.

  13. அளவுக்கு மிஞ்சினால் அமிர்தமும் நஞ்சு

    alavukku minjinaal amirdhamum nanju

    Literally: Beyond the right measure, even nectar is poison.

    Meaning: Too much of anything, even something good, does harm.

  14. ஆற்றில் போட்டாலும் அளந்து போடு

    aatril pottaalum alandhu podu

    Literally: Even if you are throwing it into the river, measure it first.

    Meaning: Be careful and thoughtful with money and resources, however you use them.

  15. கூழானாலும் குளித்துக் குடி, கந்தையானாலும் கசக்கிக் கட்டு

    koozhaanaalum kuliththuk kudi, kandhaiyaanaalum kasakkik kattu

    Literally: Even if it is only porridge, bathe before you drink it; even if it is only a rag, wash it before you wear it.

    Meaning: Whatever you have, keep yourself clean and live with dignity.
