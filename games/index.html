Traditional Games

பாரம்பரிய விளையாட்டுகள்

Games that Tamil families have played on verandas and temple floors for centuries. Play Dhayam right here, and learn how to play the others at home.

Play online

Dhayam தாயம்

The classic Tamil race game played with Dayakattai stick dice. Bring your coins out with a Dhayam, cut your opponent's coins, and race round the cloth board into the oval. Play a friend on the same screen or take on the computer.

    Aadu Puli Aattam

    ஆடு புலி ஆட்டம்

    Three hungry tigers against fifteen clever goats: a Tamil strategy game where teamwork can beat strength.

  • How to play

    Pallanguzhi

    பல்லாங்குழி

    The classic Tamil game of pits and seeds. Sow, count and capture your way to the biggest pile of seeds.

  • How to play

    Paramapadam

    பரமபதம்

    The traditional snakes-and-ladders game where good deeds lift you up, bad habits pull you down, and the goal is Vishnu's abode.
