Stories for Kids & Families

கதைகள்

Short stories to read aloud together, each with a simple lesson and a few questions to talk about afterwards.

Deity stories தெய்வக் கதைகள்

Stories of gods and devotees, told in Tamil homes for generations.

  • Ages 5+

    Avvaiyar and the Cowherd Boy

    ஔவையாரும் சுட்ட பழமும்

    A wise old poet, a cheeky boy in a jamun tree and a riddle about 'roasted fruit'. But who is the boy, really?

  • Ages 5+

    The Fruit of Wisdom

    ஞானப் பழம்

    Sage Narada brings one golden mango to Kailash, and two brothers race for it: one around the world, one around his parents.

Tales of great kings மன்னர் கதைகள்

Legends of generous and just rulers from Tamil history and tradition.

  • Ages 7+

    Manu Needhi Chozhan and the Bell of Justice

    மனுநீதிச் சோழன்

    In Thiruvarur, a grieving cow rings the Bell of Justice, and a Chola king must choose between his beloved son and what is right.

  • Ages 5+

    Paari and the Jasmine Creeper

    முல்லைக்குத் தேர் கொடுத்த பாரி

    Vel Paari was famous for his giving heart, and one day he gave his royal chariot to a tiny jasmine creeper.

Folk tales நாட்டுப்புறக் கதைகள்

Clever, funny tales loved across South India.

  • Ages 5+

    Tenali Raman and the Thieves

    தெனாலி ராமனும் திருடர்களும்

    Thieves hide outside Tenali Raman's house one hot summer night, and end up doing all his garden work for him.
