Thirukkural

திருக்குறள்

Thiruvalluvar's 1,330 couplets on how to live well, in three books — Virtue, Wealth and Love — and 133 chapters of ten kurals each. Choose a book and a chapter, or go straight to a kural by its number. Every kural has its Tamil meaning, an English meaning and G. U. Pope's verse, and can be saved as a picture to share. Start at chapter 1
