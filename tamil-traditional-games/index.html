Traditional Games

பாரம்பரிய விளையாட்டுகள்

Play the games Tamil families have loved for centuries.

  • Play online

    Dhayam தாயம்

    The classic race game with Dayakattai sticks. Bring your tokens out with a Dhayam, cut your opponent and race to the oval.

    Play now →Rules

  • Play online

    Paramapadam பரமபதம்

    The Tamil snakes and ladders of Vaikunta Ekadasi. Climb the ladders, dodge the snakes, and race to square 132.

    Play now →Rules

  • Coming soon

    Tanjavur Dhayam தஞ்சாவூர் தாயம்

    Dhayam for up to four players on a cross-shaped board, each with a home in one corner.

    Rules and the game are on their way.

  • Coming soon

    Pallanguzhi பல்லாங்குழி

    The game of pits and seeds. Sow, count and capture your way to the biggest pile of seeds.

    Read the rules

  • Coming soon

    Aadu Puli Aattam ஆடு புலி ஆட்டம்

    Three hungry tigers against fifteen clever goats — a strategy game where teamwork can beat strength.

    Read the rules
