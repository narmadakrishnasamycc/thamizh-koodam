Deities & Devotion

தெய்வங்கள் & பக்தி

Meet the deities worshipped in Tamil homes and temples — their names and symbols, the great temples of Tamil Nadu, the festivals that honour them, and simple ways families pray at home.

  • Amman, the Divine Mother

    அம்மன்

    Meet Amman, the Divine Mother of Tamil homes and villages: gentle Meenakshi, loving Kamakshi, healing Mariamman and more.

  • Lord Ganesha

    விநாயகர்

    Meet Pillaiyar, the elephant-faced god of good beginnings, prayed to first before every puja, journey and new start.

  • Lord Murugan

    முருகன்

    Meet Murugan, the young Tamil god of courage and wisdom, with his shining Vel, his peacock and his six sacred abodes.

  • Lord Shiva

    சிவன்

    Discover Shiva, the great Lord of Tamil temples: Nataraja of Chidambaram, the five element shrines and the songs of the Nayanmars.

  • Lord Vishnu

    திருமால்

    Meet Perumal, the protector of the world, praised by the Alvars and worshipped at Srirangam and the 108 Divya Desams.
