Festivals

  • Aadi Perukku

    ஆடிப்பெருக்கு

    On the 18th day of Aadi, Tamil families gather at riverbanks to thank the swelling Kaveri and other rivers for water and harvests.

  • Deepavali

    தீபாவளி

    Tamil Nadu celebrates Deepavali at dawn with an oil bath, new clothes, sweets, crackers and the story of Krishna's victory over Narakasura.

  • Kanda Sashti

    கந்த சஷ்டி

    Six days of fasting and prayer to Murugan after Deepavali, ending in Soorasamharam, the victory over Soorapadman, at Thiruchendur.

  • Karthigai Deepam

    கார்த்திகை தீபம்

    Tamil Nadu's own festival of lamps, when homes glow with clay lamps and the Maha Deepam is lit on the hill at Thiruvannamalai.

  • Navaratri

    நவராத்திரி

    Nine nights honouring Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswathi, with golu doll steps, sundal, songs, Ayudha Pooja and Vijayadasami.

  • Pongal

    பொங்கல்

    Tamil Nadu's four-day harvest festival, thanking the sun, the land and the cattle with pots of freshly boiled pongal.

  • Tamil New Year

    தமிழ்ப் புத்தாண்டு

    Puthandu welcomes the Tamil year on Chithirai 1 with kolam, temple visits, new clothes and a pachadi that holds all of life's tastes.

  • Thaipusam

    தைப்பூசம்

    A great festival of Murugan on Poosam star in Thai, when devotees carry kavadi and milk pots to Palani, Batu Caves and beyond.

  • Varalakshmi Vratham

    வரலட்சுமி விரதம்

    On a Friday before the Aavani full moon, women worship Lakshmi as Varalakshmi, the giver of boons, in a kalasam dressed as the goddess.

Deities & Devotion

  • Amman, the Divine Mother

    அம்மன்

    Meet Amman, the Divine Mother of Tamil homes and villages: gentle Meenakshi, loving Kamakshi, healing Mariamman and more.

  • Lord Ganesha

    விநாயகர்

    Meet Pillaiyar, the elephant-faced god of good beginnings, prayed to first before every puja, journey and new start.

  • Lord Murugan

    முருகன்

    Meet Murugan, the young Tamil god of courage and wisdom, with his shining Vel, his peacock and his six sacred abodes.

  • Lord Shiva

    சிவன்

    Discover Shiva, the great Lord of Tamil temples: Nataraja of Chidambaram, the five element shrines and the songs of the Nayanmars.

  • Lord Vishnu

    திருமால்

    Meet Perumal, the protector of the world, praised by the Alvars and worshipped at Srirangam and the 108 Divya Desams.

Stories

  • Avvaiyar and the Cowherd Boy

    ஔவையாரும் சுட்ட பழமும்

    A wise old poet, a cheeky boy in a jamun tree and a riddle about 'roasted fruit'. But who is the boy, really?

  • Manu Needhi Chozhan and the Bell of Justice

    மனுநீதிச் சோழன்

    In Thiruvarur, a grieving cow rings the Bell of Justice, and a Chola king must choose between his beloved son and what is right.

  • Paari and the Jasmine Creeper

    முல்லைக்குத் தேர் கொடுத்த பாரி

    Vel Paari was famous for his giving heart, and one day he gave his royal chariot to a tiny jasmine creeper.

  • Tenali Raman and the Thieves

    தெனாலி ராமனும் திருடர்களும்

    Thieves hide outside Tenali Raman's house one hot summer night, and end up doing all his garden work for him.

  • The Fruit of Wisdom

    ஞானப் பழம்

    Sage Narada brings one golden mango to Kailash, and two brothers race for it: one around the world, one around his parents.

Game guides

  • Aadu Puli Aattam

    ஆடு புலி ஆட்டம்

    Three hungry tigers against fifteen clever goats: a Tamil strategy game where teamwork can beat strength.

  • Pallanguzhi

    பல்லாங்குழி

    The classic Tamil game of pits and seeds. Sow, count and capture your way to the biggest pile of seeds.

  • Paramapadam

    பரமபதம்

    The traditional snakes-and-ladders game where good deeds lift you up, bad habits pull you down, and the goal is Vishnu's abode.

Recipes

  • Maanga Pachadi

    மாங்காய்ப் பச்சடி

    Raw mango simmered with jaggery and neem flowers: a sweet, sour, bitter and spicy dish made for Tamil New Year.

  • Sakkarai Pongal

    சர்க்கரைப் பொங்கல்

    Rice and moong dal slow-cooked with jaggery, ghee and cardamom: the sweet, golden heart of the Thai Pongal celebration.

  • Sweet Kozhukattai

    இனிப்புக் கொழுக்கட்டை

    Steamed rice-flour dumplings filled with coconut and jaggery: the sweet modak-style offering made for Vinayagar Chathurthi.

  • Ven Pongal

    வெண்பொங்கல்

    Soft, savoury rice and moong dal tempered with ghee, black pepper, cumin, ginger and cashews: a comforting Tamil breakfast.
